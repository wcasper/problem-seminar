The following is a suggested twelve week training schedule to prepare for the Putnam Exam using the textbook
**Putnam and Beyond** (PB).

---

## Week 1 – Proofs & Inequalities
**Reading**
- *Putnam and Beyond* (PB) §1.1–1.3 (Basic proof techniques, induction).
- PB §2.1 (Inequalities, AM–GM).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 1 Problems 1–10.
- PB: Ch. 2 Problems 1–6.
- Past Putnam: 2000 A1, 2002 A1.

---

## Week 2 – More Inequalities & Modular Arithmetic
**Reading**
- PB §2.2–2.3 (Cauchy–Schwarz, rearrangement).
- PB §3.1 (Divisibility, modular arithmetic basics).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 2 Problems 7–15.
- PB: Ch. 3 Problems 1–10.
- Past Putnam: 2011 A1, 2014 A1.

---

## Week 3 – Number Theory (Diophantine Equations)
**Reading**
- PB §3.2–3.3 (Diophantine equations, gcd/Euclidean algorithm).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 3 Problems 11–20.
- Past Putnam: 2001 A1, 2009 A1.

---

## Week 4 – Combinatorics I
**Reading**
- PB §4.1–4.2 (Counting, binomial coefficients, pigeonhole principle).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 4 Problems 1–15.
- Past Putnam: 2003 A1, 2012 A1.

**Checkpoint**
- 1-hour quiz (3 problems: 1 inequality, 1 modular arithmetic, 1 combinatorial).

---

## Week 5 – Combinatorics II & Intro to Vectors
**Reading**
- PB §4.3–4.4 (Invariants, recursion in combinatorics).
- PB §5.1 (Vectors, dot product, orthogonality).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 4 Problems 16–25.
- PB: Ch. 5 Problems 1–8.
- Past Putnam: 1999 A1, 2013 A1.

---

## Week 6 – Matrices & Geometry
**Reading**
- PB §5.2–5.3 (Determinants, eigenvalues).
- PB §6.1 (Coordinate geometry, transformations).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 5 Problems 9–20.
- PB: Ch. 6 Problems 1–8.
- Past Putnam: 2007 A1, 2010 A1.

**Mini Mock**
- 2 hours, 3 problems (one from algebra, one from combinatorics, one from geometry).

---

## Week 7 – Series & Improper Integrals
**Reading**
- PB §7.1–7.3 (Series convergence tests, telescoping sums, bounding).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 7 Problems 1–15.
- Past Putnam: 2005 A1, 2018 A1.

---

## Week 8 – Functions & Problem-Solving Tricks
**Reading**
- PB §8.1–8.2 (Functional equations, bounding tricks).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 8 Problems 1–10.
- Past Putnam: 2008 A1, 2019 A1.

**Practice**
- Weekly 2-hour session (3 problems, timed).

---

## Week 9 – Mixed Topic Problems
**Reading**
- PB §9.1–9.2 (Mixed strategies: invariants, parity, symmetry).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 9 Problems 1–15.
- Past Putnam: 1994 B1, 2004 B1.

**Practice**
- Full mock exam (12 problems from Putnam 2000).

---

## Week 10 – More Mixed Practice
**Reading**
- PB §10.1–10.2 (Strategic approaches: simplifying, testing small cases).

**Problems**
- PB: Ch. 10 Problems 1–15.
- Past Putnam: 2015 A1, 2017 A1.

**Assignment**
- Write 3 *clean*, full solutions and peer-review in group session.

---

## Week 11 – Exam Simulation
**Focus**
- Time management, clarity of writing, partial credit strategy.

**Practice**
- Full mock exam: Putnam 2019 (6 hours, all 12 problems).
- Debrief: each student picks 2 problems they attempted and writes full clean solutions.

---

## Week 12 – Final Review
**Focus**
- Light review, confidence, and strategy reinforcement.

**Activities**
- Review key tools: AM–GM, pigeonhole, induction, modular arithmetic.
- Mock exam: Putnam 2022 (6 hours).
- End with relaxed problem-solving games to keep morale high.

---

## Expected Outcomes
By exam day, students will have:
- Read ~10 chapters of *Putnam and Beyond*.
- Solved ~150 targeted problems.
- Taken 3 full-length mock exams.
- Built confidence to attempt 6–8 problems on the real exam with solid partial solutions.
